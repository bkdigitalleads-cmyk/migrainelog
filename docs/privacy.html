Migraine Log

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Migraine Log ("the app", on the App Store as Migraine Tracker for Headaches) is a headache and migraine diary for iPhone. It is made by Brian Kershner and built so that your health information stays on your device.

What you log

Attacks, their times and length, severity and pain, symptoms, triggers, medications and doses, headache-free days, notes and the name you may put on a report are saved only in the app's storage on your iPhone. They are not uploaded, synced or sent to us or anyone else. The app has no account system, no server and works offline. It does not use your location, the camera, contacts, health records or the weather.

Reports and exports

The PDF report and the CSV file are created on your iPhone and go only where you choose to send them (Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop, a printer and so on).

Purchases

If you buy Migraine Log Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to confirm purchases. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous identifier and your purchase history for this app, never your name or anything you logged. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the app schedules one local reminder on your iPhone the day before the trial ends. It is created on the device. No push service is used and nothing is sent from a server. The evening question "Headache today?" is shown inside the app, not as a notification.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect usage data. On the App Store the app is listed as Data Not Collected.

Deleting your data

Delete any attack from its details, use Settings, Delete everything I logged, or delete the app. Everything it stored goes with it.

Not medical advice

Migraine Log is a personal log. It does not diagnose, treat, or give medical or dosing advice, and it does not predict attacks. Talk to your doctor about your headaches and your medications.
