  - Noticed, not changed:
    - Cold start from the quick action: Expo 57's scene delegate hands the shortcut to the app delegate before JavaScript listens, and expo-quick-actions only records `initial` under the old app-delegate launch path, so from a cold start the action probably opens Home without starting the attack (from the background it works). Fixing it needs a small native change (config plugin or a patched module); left for 1.0.1 after checking on a device. The migrainelog://start link does work from a cold start.
    - Siri and App Intents: an App Intent ("Log a migraine") needs Swift in the app target through a config plugin, which cannot be compiled or checked here and would risk a paid build; noted for 1.0.1. Until then: a Shortcut with Open URL migrainelog://start.
    - expo-quick-actions 6.0.2 lists SDK 54 as its newest; its Swift was read against SDK 57's ExpoAppDelegateSubscriber and scene forwarding (both present), but only the EAS build proves it compiles.
    - Barometric pressure (WeatherKit) for a later version; 1.0.0 has no weather or network features.
    - ICHD-3 also has a rule for several acute classes together (10 or more days a month in total); the brief asked for the per-type levels only, so that is what shows.
    - The footer line ends each part of the PDF (summary, calendar, attack list) rather than sitting at the bottom of every printed page: fixed-position footers print differently in WebKit and Chromium. A long attack list over several pages has it once at the end.
    - "Other" medications count as acute medication days (they are taken for an attack); preventives never do.
    - Age rating: the honest answer to the medical information question is probably "Infrequent/Mild", which gives 12+/13+ rather than 4+; store/metadata.md lays out both and asks Brian.
    - The keywords include "migraines" and "headaches", plurals of name words; Apple matches plurals on its own, so these two slots could go to new words in 1.0.1.
    - The website's App Store link points at apps.apple.com until the app id exists. GitHub Pages has to be switched on (Settings, Pages, main, /docs) before the privacy and support links work.
    - In the renders the switches are the browser's teal and the date pickers are drawn stand-ins; on iOS they are the system's controls in the app's colors.
    - `npm test` prints Node's MODULE_TYPELESS_PACKAGE_JSON warning (the engine does too); harmless.
